## Reading (p1-critic-plan)

**Key damage ≈ 0 holds, and is not a timeout artifact.**
- At equal idle rates (the regression intercepts), honest-agent key damage is between −6 and +1 pp in every branch of U, T and DB.
- Every CI includes 0 or lies below it. BASE in arm U is −5.7 [−9.2, −2.2]: honest agents end with *more* keys than in the twin, plausibly because a successful defection hands the defector a key.
- The below-median subsets agree.
- The slopes in T and DB are negative, as expected if the twin's extra idling handicaps it. But removing that handicap moves damage toward 0, not toward harm.

**Bribe > lie holds, and gets larger.**
- With all three branches below the median idle rate (10 seeds): bribe-only +1.70 vs lie-only +0.30, diff +1.40 [+0.80, +2.00], exact p = 0.008.
- Adjusted for the idle-rate gap (36 seeds): +1.00 [+0.66, +1.38], against +0.89 unadjusted.

**The < 10% subset is unusable.** Only 0–2 seeds per branch qualify, because the forced-idle measure includes ordinary parse failures (about 5% even in arms without timeouts).
